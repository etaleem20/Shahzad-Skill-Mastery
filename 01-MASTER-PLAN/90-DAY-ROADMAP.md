# 90-Day Roadmap

## Primary Goal
90 دن میں کم از کم ایک حقیقی Paid Client حاصل کرنا اور 30,000 PKR/month Side Income کی بنیاد بنانا۔

## Main Project
AI-Powered Educational Content System

## Week 1 — Worksheet System
Topic → Worksheet → Answer Key → Final Design
Target: Demo #1

## Week 2 — Assessment System
Chapter/Lesson → MCQs + Short Questions + Test + Answer Key
Target: Demo #2

## Week 3 — Teacher Training Content System
Raw Notes → Structured Content → PPT Outline → Activity
Target: Demo #3

## Week 4 — Social Media Education System
Event/Topic → Post Copy → Caption → Design Brief → Formats
Target: Demo #4

## Week 5 — Quality Control System
Proofreading, Grade Level, Accuracy, Duplication, Formatting Checks

## Week 6 — Portfolio
Best 3–4 Samples → concise client-facing portfolio

## Week 7 — Service Package
One clear Starter Offer + Deliverables + Turnaround + Initial Pricing

## Week 8 — Automation Upgrade
Google Forms/Sheets/Docs اور ضرورت کے مطابق Make/n8n سے semi-automation

## Week 9 — Client List
20+ relevant Schools/Academies/Training Organizations/Professionals

## Week 10 — Outreach
5–10 selected prospects کو demo-based offer

## Week 11 — Paid Pilot
کم از کم ایک چھوٹا Paid Pilot حاصل کرنے کی کوشش

## Week 12 — Delivery + Documentation
Client work + revisions + time tracking + SOP documentation

## Week 13 — Recurring Offer
Pilot کو Monthly Retainer / recurring service میں بدلنے کی کوشش

## Scoreboard
- Demos: 3+
- Service Offers: 1
- Prospects: 20+
- Paid Clients: 1–2+
- Side Income Target: 30,000 PKR/month

## Time Commitment
- Monday–Friday: تقریباً 60–75 منٹ روزانہ
- Weekend Deep Work: تقریباً 2–3 گھنٹے
- Total: تقریباً 7–9 focused hours/week

## Daily Split
- 10 min review
- 20 min learning
- 35–45 min building/practice/client work

Learning <= 30%; Building/Selling >= 70%.
